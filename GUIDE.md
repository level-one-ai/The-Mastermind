# The Mastermind: the plain-English guide

## What it does

You tell Claude which businesses you want to ring. The Mastermind looks each one up, finds a real reason to call them, and writes the whole call for that business: what to say if someone else answers, your opener, why you're calling, two or three questions, how to ask for the meeting, answers to the things they'll push back with, your voicemail, and the text to send after.

Every script is written for one company. Their name, their town and the reason are already in it. Nothing to fill in.

## How to use it

1. **Ask for scripts.** For example: "Mastermind, write scripts for these five plumbers in Dalkeith" and paste names or websites. Or: "Find me 10 electricians in Midlothian to ring." It shows you the list first and only writes scripts for the ones you pick.
2. **Open the Call Desk** and press **Check for new scripts**.
3. **Before you ring**, check the number against TPS / CTPS and tick the box. (Most trades are sole traders, who use TPS.)
4. **Ring, read, log.** After each call press Booked, Callback, No answer or Lost. If it's Lost, say why and where it went wrong. It moves to the Lost list.
5. **Edit anything** that doesn't sound like you and save it. That's how it learns your voice.
6. **Next time** you ask for scripts, it reads everything you logged first.

## How it learns

- Each niche has two starting templates: **A** asks permission ("This is a cold call, can I have 30 seconds?") and **B** goes straight to the reason. They take turns.
- The Stats page shows the **booking rate** for each: meetings booked ÷ (booked + lost). Wrong numbers don't count against a script.
- Once a template has 10 booked-or-lost leads, the better one becomes the **champion** and gets most new scripts. Every third script tests a **challenger** that changes one thing, picked from your results: if most losses say "went wrong at the opener", the challenger tries a new opener; if "already have someone" keeps coming up, it tries a better answer for that.
- A template that books nothing in 15 leads is retired.
- Small numbers lie. Under 10 leads it says "too early" instead of guessing.

## Things it will never do

- Leave blanks in a script.
- Make up a reason to call, a review, a name or a number.
- Say "AI" or "automation" on a call, or name a client.
- Give a price on a cold call.
- Keep pushing after someone says "don't call me again". That lead goes to Lost as Do not call and is never called again.

## Practise

Say "drill me before I call" and it plays the owner, throws the likely brush-offs at you, and tells you what to read again before you dial.
