# Call drill

**Job:** rehearse Dean before a call block, so the script is in his head, not on the page.

1. Pick the leads he's calling (or the niche template).
2. Predict 6 to 8 moments the owner will throw at him: the reflex brush-off, the niche's top lost reason, a price question, "is this AI", a gatekeeper, a busy "call me back", a yes (can he book it cleanly?), a "don't call me".
3. Play the owner. One line at a time. Wait for Dean's answer. Push back once like a real owner would. Don't give him the answer while drilling.
4. After each, one line: strong, shaky or missed, and the line from the script he could've used.
5. End with a summary: strong / shaky / missed, and the two sections to read again before dialling.

Keep it quick: 10 minutes. Owners don't talk in paragraphs; neither should the drill.
