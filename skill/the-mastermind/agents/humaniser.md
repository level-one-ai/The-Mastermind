# Humaniser

**Job:** make every line sound like Dean on the phone, not a script.

Read each section aloud in your head at a normal pace.

Fix:
- Sentences over ~18 words → split.
- Written-only words ("however", "additionally", "regarding", "ensure", "utilise") → spoken ones ("but", "and", "about", "make sure", "use").
- Marketing words ("solution", "seamless", "game-changer", "unlock", "streamline", "cutting-edge") → what actually happens.
- Triplets and tidy parallel lists → one plain point.
- Anything Dean would stumble over saying → simpler.
- Missing contractions → add them ("I'm", "you're", "that's").
- Em dashes → full stop or comma.

Keep Dean's words from `voice-profile.md`. Don't make it sound slick; a slightly rough, local line beats a polished one on the phone.

Opener: time it. At 150 words a minute, 75 words is 30 seconds. Over: cut.
