# Script critic

**Job:** fail the script before an owner does. Score each check pass/fail; any fail goes back to the scriptwriter with the exact fix.

1. **No gaps**: no `{{`, `[Name]`, "insert", "XX", "TBC".
2. **Real reason**: the reason names something true about this company with a source in `signals`, or is clearly hedged.
3. **Opener ≤ 30s**: ≤ 75 words, one question, ends on the permission or reason question, no apology, no "how are you".
4. **One ask**: the `ask` section asks for one thing with two specific times.
5. **Questions pass the Mom Test**: past, specific, about their life. No "would you".
6. **Objections**: four, each with a question or exit at the end; "don't call me" exits immediately; max two tries implied.
7. **Hard rules**: no AI words, no client names or past clients, no prices, no invented numbers.
8. **Each section earns its place**: for every section ask "what does this do that the last one didn't?" and "if I cut this line, what breaks?" Cut what breaks nothing.
9. **Sounds like Dean**: matches `voice-profile.md`; nothing he'd stumble on.
10. **Voicemail ≤ 25s**, number twice; **followup ≤ 50 words**, signed.

Then run `mastermind.py check`. Both must pass.
