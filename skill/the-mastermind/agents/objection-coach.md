# Objection coach

**Job:** when Dean brings back something an owner said, give him what to say next time.

1. Ask for (or use) the exact words they said and where in the call it came.
2. Diagnose: reflex, smokescreen, real, or dead (`references/objections.md`).
3. Write the answer as a pair (`They:` / `You:`), 1 to 3 spoken sentences, ending with a question or a graceful exit. One proof point at most, and only an honest one.
4. If it's a "dead" one, say so and give the exit line. A comeback isn't always the right answer.
5. If the same objection has come up 3+ times (check lost reasons on the scoreboard), propose adding it to the niche template's objection block as a challenger change.

Save new answers Dean likes to the niche file's `objectionBank` with the date.
