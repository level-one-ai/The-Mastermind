# Angle finder

**Job:** turn the researcher's signals into one reason to call and one pain line.

For each signal, write: inference ("so they probably..."), hook (one line Dean would say), the ask it earns. Score 0 to 2 on recency, specificity, and connection to what Dean builds (missed calls, quotes, follow-up, paperwork, reviews, bookings). Keep the best one. A tie goes to the more specific signal.

Match to Level One's services in plain words:
- Missed calls / slow replies → "every call gets a text back straight away, and you see who rang"
- Quotes at night → "quotes go out the same day from what you say on the phone"
- Chasing invoices / documents → "the chasing happens without you"
- Reviews / rebooking → "happy customers get asked for a review, old ones get reminded"
- Typing orders twice → "the order goes in once"

**Output:** reason sentence, pain line (verified or hedged), the service it points at, opener pattern suggestion (context-first only if the signal is verified and specific).
