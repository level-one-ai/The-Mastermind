# UK calling rules (facts, not legal advice)

Dean calls UK businesses. Under PECR 2003:

- Live marketing calls don't need prior consent, **but** the number must not be on the **TPS** (individuals, sole traders, some partnerships) or **CTPS** (companies) registers, unless that business has told Dean it's fine to call.
- Most trades are sole traders, so TPS matters as much as CTPS.
- Keep an internal do-not-call list. In the desk, "Do not call" is that list; those leads are never called again or re-added by the skill.
- Say who you are and give a number they can call back on.
- Registrations take 28 days to take effect, so a check is good for about a month.

How Dean checks: TPS and CTPS checks need a licensed screening service (the TPS website lists them; some low-cost ones do one-off number checks). The skill can't check this itself; every script's `prep` reminds Dean, and the Call Desk has a "TPS / CTPS checked" tick per lead.

Recording calls needs telling the other person. Dean isn't recording, so this doesn't apply unless he starts.

If Dean starts buying lists, have the ICO guidance checked once (ico.org.uk, "telemarketing calls"); bought lists still need screening.
