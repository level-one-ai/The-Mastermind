# Questions (the Mom Test on the phone)

After the opener and reason, Dean has earned two or three questions. They're about the owner's life and what already happened, never about Level One or what they "would" do.

## Rules

1. **Their life, not Dean's idea.** Don't describe the build until they've told you the problem is real.
2. **Specifics in the past.** "When did that last happen?" beats "Is that ever a problem?"
3. **Talk less.** Ask, then stop. Silence is fine.
4. A question fails if the answer is "yes" whether or not they'd ever buy. "Would it help if your phone texted people back?" fails. "What happened the last time you missed a call on a job?" passes.

## Question bank by niche (pick two, maybe three)

**Trades**
- Q: When you're on a job and the phone goes, what happens to that call?
- Q: Roughly how many quotes are you sending a week, and when do you do them?
- Q: Last time a customer went quiet after a quote, what did you do?

**Local services**
- Q: What happens when someone rings while you're with a customer?
- Q: How many no-shows did you have last week, would you say?
- Q: How do you get people to come back or leave a review just now?

**Property**
- Q: When an enquiry comes in at 8 at night, when does it get answered?
- Q: How are tenants reporting repairs just now?

**Professional services**
- Q: When you take on a new client, how long until you've got everything you need from them?
- Q: How much of your week goes on chasing documents?

**B2B distribution**
- Q: When an order comes in by email, how many times does someone type it?
- Q: How long does a quote take to go out at the moment?

## Listen for

Write a `Listen for:` line after the questions. The signal Dean wants is a **cost they've already paid**: a lost job, an evening on quotes, a named person doing the typing. Compliments ("that sounds clever") and futures ("we'd definitely look at that") are not signals.

## Commitment, not compliments

The call ends with a clear advance (the meeting) or a clear no. A polite "send me something" with no answer to the qualifying question is a zombie lead, logged as Asked for info, not as a win.
