# Price and concessions

## On the cold call

No numbers. The goal is the meeting. If pushed twice, give the shape honestly: "a one-off build fee and then a small monthly to keep it running and improving." Then the ask.

Splitting the money question when it comes up in the meeting:
"Is it that the number doesn't fit right now, or that you're not sure it'd be worth it?"
- Not sure it's worth it: rebuild the value from their own words ("you said you lose two jobs a week").
- Genuinely no money: change the terms, not the price.

## Concession order (for the meeting, not the call)

Every give gets a get. Never discount unilaterally.

| Give (cheap to Dean, valued by them) | Get back |
|---|---|
| Start smaller: one thing built first (missed-call text-back), the rest later | A start date this month |
| Phased payments on the build | Monthly retainer from month one |
| A month's retainer free | A written testimonial with a money figure if it works |
| Dean's time to set it up on site | An intro to two other business owners |

Walk-away: if they want it built for nothing or need Dean to support something he can't build himself, it's a no with a "when that changes, give me a ring".

The testimonial with a money figure is the most valuable get Dean can ask for right now.
