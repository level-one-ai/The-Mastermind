# Call cadence

## Attempts per lead

| Attempt | When | Voicemail? | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1, best window for the niche | Yes | Text within 5 min |
| 2 | Day 3, the other window | No | Nothing |
| 3 | Day 6 | Yes, different message | Text or email |
| 4 | Day 10 | No | If still nothing, park: callback in 60 days |

93% of conversations happen by the third attempt (Cognism 2025). After four with no conversation the lead is parked, not lost; it hasn't said no.

## Call blocks

- 60 to 90 minutes, phone only. Close email.
- Prep the list before the block (scripts written, TPS ticked); dial during it.
- Log every call in the desk straight away. Unlogged calls don't teach the skill anything.
- After the block, note in one line what felt off. The analyst reads call notes.

## Stop rules

- "Don't call me again": Do not call, immediately. Never re-added.
- Wrong number or closed down: Wrong number. Find the right number before re-adding.
- Booked: stop calling; the meeting is the next step.
